\subsection{Evidence boundary and scientific question}

Phase~1, Phase~2, and Phase~3 are immutable completed results. Phase~3B was not a retrospective rescue of the failed 64-step gate: it changed the suffix horizon, used a new manifest and output root, and remains developmental evidence. The scientific question was whether the same snapshots, candidate seeds, policy, trigger definitions, nominal controls, and fallback schedules contained useful causal recovery variation when each branch received enough genuine policy replans to complete its active stage.

Let $Y_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether explicit candidate $j\in\{1,\ldots,4\}$ completes snapshot $i$'s frozen live trigger predicate within the task-specific horizon, with full-task success also counted as completion. Let $Y_i^{\mathrm{nominal}}$ be the agreeing same-seed nominal continuation. Define
\begin{align}
  M_i &= \mathbf{1}\!\left\{\min_jY_{ij}<\max_jY_{ij}\right\},\\
  R_i^{\mathrm{random}} &= \frac{1}{4}\sum_{j=1}^{4}Y_{ij},
  &R_i^{\mathrm{oracle}} &= \max_jY_{ij},\\
  O_i &= R_i^{\mathrm{oracle}}-R_i^{\mathrm{random}},
  &G_i &= R_i^{\mathrm{oracle}}-Y_i^{\mathrm{nominal}},\\
  B_i &= R_i^{\mathrm{random}}-Y_i^{\mathrm{nominal}}.
\end{align}
$M_i$ measures rankable variation, $O_i$ is finite-set ranking headroom over random selection, $G_i$ tests whether even the oracle candidate improves over simply continuing, and $B_i$ separates generic proposal or replanning benefit from ranking benefit. Oracle-best-of-four is an optimistic finite-seed upper bound, not a deployable success estimate.

\subsection{Phase 3B-0: four-snapshot engineering sentinel}

Before inspecting any extended outcome, deterministically choose the lowest-reset-index valid parent from each of ArrangeTea and CuttingToolSelection, retaining its prefix and landmark snapshots. This gives four snapshots. For each snapshot, execute the same four explicit candidate seeds, two same-seed nominal repeats, and one environment-only control, for $4\times7=28$ branches.

Use 480 environment steps for ArrangeTea (30 genuine replans) and 256 for CuttingToolSelection (16 genuine replans). The sentinel passes only if:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the frozen Phase~2 source-plan, payload, snapshot, runtime, code, and checkpoint hashes match;
  \item the deterministic snapshot selection and exact recorded candidate and continuation seeds match the registered manifest;
  \item every extended branch reproduces its corresponding Phase~2 first 64 requests, actions, observations, causal environment transitions, and semantic traces exactly;
  \item same-seed nominal repeats agree exactly, every requested artifact is complete, and there are zero branch errors;
  \item each task receives its registered horizon and the runner stops only at stage completion, full-task success, terminal failure, safety termination, or that horizon.
\end{enumerate}

An integrity failure stops collection and requires repair followed by a new output root; it does not count as negative recovery evidence. The failed sentinel and error ledger remain preserved.

\subsection{Developmental continuation gates}

Local candidate-critic work becomes eligible only if the full 20-snapshot screen satisfies all of the following planning thresholds:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item at least $4/20$ snapshots have mixed candidate outcomes, arising from at least three independent parents and both tasks;
  \item task-macro oracle-over-random headroom is at least 15 percentage points;
  \item task-macro oracle-over-nominal headroom is positive in both tasks and approximately at least 10 percentage points overall;
  \item there is no catastrophic safety violation and the task-macro increase in prior-stage regression or declared safety harm is at most five percentage points relative to nominal;
  \item apparent immediate completions retain their benefit at the next stage landmark or common terminal horizon.
\end{enumerate}

These are compute-allocation thresholds, not venue requirements or confirmatory tests. If $0/20$ snapshots are mixed, the simple independent-binomial one-sided 95\% upper bound is approximately 0.14 and $P(0/20\mid p=0.20)\approx0.0115$. Because the 20 snapshots are nested within 10 parents and only two tasks, report those calculations as descriptive sensitivity, not population confidence.

\subsection{Fallback: bounded higher-level operator-headroom screen}

The local-candidate gate selected this follow-up, which has now completed. The executed design compared \path{continue}, \path{history_reset_replan}, \path{stage_retry}, and \path{retreat_reset_replan} on the same 20 snapshots, with two paired continuation-seed schedules, for 160 scientific suffixes. Cache-only flush was replaced by history reset because the inference-boundary snapshots had an empty cache. Stage retry used fixed supervisor-supplied goals. The earlier planned 20 oracle-rewind branches were omitted because genuine earlier complete anchors were unavailable; no 180-branch or rewind result is claimed.

All engineering gates passed, but continuation reached 22.5\% stage completion versus 17.5\% for every intervention, oracle operator routing added zero completion gain, and full-task success was zero. The completed result and practical-gate failure are detailed in Section~\ref{sec:operator-screen}. The resulting decision is \path{mechanism_redesign_or_earlier_snapshot_study}, not router training or broad recoverability-surface collection.

At most one separately registered common-stage-entry timing diagnostic is proposed in Section~\ref{sec:next-timing-study}. It must use real earlier simulator-policy states, paired nominal prefixes, and a common absolute endpoint; it cannot relabel current snapshots or claim that the failed screen established a timing mechanism.

\subsection{Conditional formal five-task screen}

Only the first row of Table~\ref{tab:phase3b-routing} unlocks the candidate-ranking screen, and the observed result did not enter that row. The formal five-task candidate screen is therefore blocked for the current proposal family. If a materially different future proposal mechanism first establishes local candidate headroom under a separately frozen pilot, the conditional design would use five tasks, 10 eventually failed and 10 eventually successful-control parents per task, and two detector-independent snapshots per parent, producing 200 snapshots and approximately 1,000 suffixes. This would remain a case-control opportunity study, not an estimate of failure prevalence.

The formal gate retains at least 20\% mixed snapshots, support in at least three of five tasks, at least 15 points of task-macro oracle-over-random headroom, positive oracle-over-nominal benefit, next-stage durability, and bounded harm. The collector must continue each nominal parent to terminal outcome before extracting the two snapshots; the current Phase~2 runner stops after its snapshot pair and therefore cannot provide the eventual failed/successful-control labels required by this design.
