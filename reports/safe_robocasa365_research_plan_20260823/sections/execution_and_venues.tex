\subsection{Execution order}

Phase~1 failed confirmation; Phase~2 passed exact replay; Phase~3 failed the short-suffix opportunity gate. Phase~3B passed engineering but found only 0.0125 oracle-over-random headroom and no full-task success. The subsequent 160-suffix higher-level operator screen also passed engineering but failed scientifically: continuation reached 22.5\% completion versus 17.5\% for each intervention and oracle routing gain was zero. The next proposed experiment is the 200-trajectory common-stage-entry timing diagnostic in Section~\ref{sec:next-timing-study}. Candidate-critic and operator-router training, broad surfaces, flow guidance, and end-to-end SAFE-triggered recovery remain blocked or deferred.

The following dependencies must remain sequential:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item exact snapshot replay before counterfactual scale;
  \item the completed engineering sentinel before the completed full training-horizon screen, with no efficacy stop based on four outcomes;
  \item preserve both completed negative local-candidate and operator screens before the single proposed timing diagnostic;
  \item operator-over-continuation and routing-over-best-fixed opportunity before critic or router training;
  \item sealed offline model gate before prospective fixed-trigger selection;
  \item prospective fixed-trigger benefit before differentiable guidance;
  \item development freeze before every fresh confirmation or final trial;
  \item healthy ordinary-policy positive control before second-policy failure collection.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Recommended paper contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item We introduce intervention-specific recoverability curves for long-horizon VLA execution, measured through exact-prefix counterfactual branches over task stages, delays, and recovery operators.
  \item We show that terminal failure likelihood is not equivalent to causal intervention value or remaining recovery time.
  \item Conditional on causal headroom, we learn the simplest justified decision mechanism---candidate critic, operator router, or utility gate---and test its prospective task-success benefit while controlling unnecessary and harmful intervention.
\end{enumerate}

Critic-guided flow is a fourth contribution only if Phase~8 passes matched-compute and held-out safety tests. It cannot be the core novelty because closely related VLA guidance has already been demonstrated~\cite{qvgm,guidedactionflow}. Otherwise, retain the simpler Phase~6 executor.
