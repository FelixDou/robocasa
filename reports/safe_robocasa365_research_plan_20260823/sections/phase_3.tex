\subsection{Scientific hypothesis}

Even a perfect critic cannot improve recovery if all candidate chunks from a snapshot lead to the same outcome. Before training, estimate the oracle headroom of the proposal set. For candidate set $\mathcal{A}_i=\{a_{i1},\ldots,a_{iK}\}$ at snapshot $i$, define
\begin{equation}
  O_i = \max_{a\in\mathcal{A}_i}Y_i(a)-\operatorname{mean}_{a\in\mathcal{A}_i}Y_i(a).
\end{equation}
$O_i>0$ means candidate selection can outperform random choice for that snapshot. The phase hypothesis is that a nontrivial fraction of early and mid-stage snapshots have mixed candidate outcomes and positive oracle headroom.

\subsection{Two-tier experimental design}

\textbf{Tier 1: completed bounded pilot.} The 20 validated Phase~2 snapshots and 80 explicit candidates were analyzed. The two same-seed repeats collapsed to one nominal control; the environment-only branch remained a replay diagnostic and was excluded from utility estimates. No checkpoint, normalization, threshold, candidate set, or outcome was updated. The frozen live trigger predicate had to be false at the snapshot and become true within the suffix, or the full task had to succeed; generic progress and branch-local ordered traces did not count as completion. The pilot failed both estimable continuation criteria and could not adjudicate the formal ``three of five tasks'' requirement.

The pilot supported scaling only if at least 20\% of snapshots had mixed candidate outcomes and task-macro oracle-best-of-four exceeded expected random-of-four completion by at least 15 percentage points. Both observed values were zero. This requires redesign before collection, not critic training.

\textbf{Tier 2: blocked conditional formal screen.} Do not run the five-task design unless the bounded horizon redesign below first establishes nonzero opportunity. If that redesign passes, use the same five development tasks and the exact Phase~2 snapshot protocol. The larger design below remains frozen before new collection.

Use the same five development tasks. For each task, collect 10 eventually failed and 10 eventually successful-control parents, producing 100 parents. This is an explicit case-control opportunity study and must never be used to estimate deployment prevalence or event rates.

From each parent, branch two states selected independently of SAFE score:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item exact active-stage prefix 2;
  \item the first inference at or beyond 25\% of a successful-stage horizon derived only from training data.
\end{enumerate}
At each of 200 snapshots, evaluate the nominal candidate and four explicitly seeded candidates. Execute the selected first chunk and then continue with one frozen fallback-policy seed schedule. The planning total is 1,000 suffixes.

The primary outcome is completion of the frozen live trigger predicate within a frozen horizon, for example a training-derived 90th percentile of successful remaining-stage duration with lower and upper caps. The predicate must be false at the snapshot and become true during the suffix, or the full task must succeed. Branch-local ordered traces restart without the parent's monotonic completed-prefix history and are diagnostic only; they must not redefine the active trigger. Full-task success, progress, regression, safety, and cost are secondary. A candidate that finishes the immediate stage but systematically destroys later task success must not be considered a successful recovery method.
