This document starts from the SAFE and recovery evidence in the repository and incorporates results through 12 October 2026. Phase~1 frozen confirmation failed; Phase~2 complete snapshot replay passed; Phases~3 and 3B found negligible local-candidate selection headroom. The subsequent 160-suffix higher-level operator screen also passed engineering but failed its scientific gate: continuation reached 22.5\% stage completion versus 17.5\% for every intervention, with zero oracle routing gain and zero full-task success. The next proposed experiment is a bounded common-stage-entry timing diagnostic, not model training or broad recovery scaling. Completed results, supplied cluster exports, proposed protocols, supervisor-conditioned operators, and deployable intervention evidence remain explicitly distinct.
