\caption{In success-terminated, time-limited benchmarks every failed rollout runs to its time
limit $H_k$, so a per-task timer that alarms once an attempt outlasts the task's successes flags
all failures. A monitor helps only if it alarms \emph{earlier}. We fuse any monitor with the timer
(first alarm wins), calibrate both at a 5\% false-alarm target, and measure the extra warning over
the timer (the fused timer can be looser than P's). Example: a failed XR-1 \emph{OpenCabinet} rollout, monitor score from SAFE.}
